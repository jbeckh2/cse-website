LOUISIANA EXPANSION

Expanding or investing in Louisiana?

Louisiana offers significant opportunities for companies creating jobs, making capital investments, developing facilities, and expanding operations. The challenge is understanding which programs, organizations, incentives, workforce resources, sites, and government processes apply to a specific project.

Callegari Solutions helps companies identify those opportunities, organize the path forward, and coordinate the public and private resources surrounding the project.

EXPLORE LOUISIANA EXPANSION SERVICES
WHERE WE HELP
  • Incentives. Identify state and local programs that may apply, and understand requirements and timing.
  • Workforce. Review training programs, hiring resources, and the organizations behind them.
  • Site Strategy. Evaluate location considerations, utilities, transportation, and jurisdictional questions.
  • Government Navigation. Determine which agencies, offices, and public processes affect the project.
  • Project Coordination. Organize stakeholders, sequence the work, and keep the project moving.
